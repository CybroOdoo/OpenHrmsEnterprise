Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Open HRMS Employee History module?

The Open HRMS Employee History module helps track an employee's past job history, internal transfers, promotions, salary changes, contract updates, and other work-related changes within a company.

Where can I find the Employee History records?

Employee history records can be found by navigating to Employees > Employee Profile > History Tab, where all past records are maintained in an organized view.

What types of history are tracked by this module?

The module tracks four key types of history: department and job position changes, salary wage changes, employee contract modifications, and timesheet hourly cost updates.

Is this module compatible with Odoo 19 Enterprise?

Yes, this module is fully compatible with Odoo 19 Enterprise and is available on Odoo.sh. It extends the standard Employee form with a dedicated History tab without disrupting any core Odoo functionality.
