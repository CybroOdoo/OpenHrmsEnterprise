Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Saudi GOSI Enterprise Module?

The Saudi GOSI Enterprise Module is an Odoo add-on developed by Cybrosys that automates the calculation and management of GOSI contributions for both employees and employers in compliance with Saudi labor laws.

Who is eligible for GOSI contributions?

Saudi citizens between the ages of 18 and 60 are eligible. The module automatically computes the employee's age from their date of birth and sets the eligibility flag accordingly.

How are GOSI contributions calculated on the payslip?

The module adds dedicated salary rules for both the employee deduction and the employer contribution. These are automatically computed on every payslip for eligible employees, following the latest GOSI regulations.

Where can I enter the employee's GOSI details?

A dedicated GOSI Information tab is added to the employee form. From there you can fill in the GOSI Type, GOSI Number, and Issued Date for each employee.
