Frequently Asked Questions

What is the purpose of the Enterprise Open HRMS Employee Disciplinary Tracking module?

The module helps organisations efficiently track, manage, and document employee disciplinary actions. It provides a structured workflow for issuing disciplinary notices, collecting employee explanations, and recording final actions to ensure transparency and compliance with HR policies.

What are the different stages in the disciplinary workflow?

The workflow has five stages: Draft (record created), Waiting Explanation (employee notified), Waiting Action (explanation submitted, awaiting manager decision), Action Validated (final action taken), and Cancelled (record cancelled). HR managers can also reset cancelled records back to Draft.

Can the module be customized to fit specific organisational policies?

Yes, the module is highly customisable. You can configure disciplinary reason categories and action categories to match your organisation's policies. The role-based access ensures only HR managers can validate or cancel actions.

How can I see all disciplinary records for a specific employee?

Open any employee's form view. A smart button labelled "Disciplinary Action" displays the total count of validated actions for that employee. Clicking it filters and shows all related disciplinary records directly.
