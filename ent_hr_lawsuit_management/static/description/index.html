Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Enterprise Open HRMS Legal Actions module?

The Enterprise Open HRMS Legal Actions module by Cybrosys Technologies is designed to help organizations manage legal actions related to employees. It allows HR departments to track and document legal cases, assign legal representatives, monitor case statuses, and maintain relevant legal records within Odoo's HRMS system.

Does the module support case status tracking?

Yes, the module allows HR managers and legal teams to track the status of ongoing legal cases. Users can update case statuses, set hearing dates, log case progress, and maintain a clear record of legal proceedings. This helps organizations stay informed and manage legal actions efficiently.

Who can access legal records in the system?

The module has two user roles: Manager Law and User Law. Managers can see all legal records across the company, while normal users can only view cases where they are the linked employee. Access is configured through Odoo's standard group and record rule system.

Can I track cases involving non-employees?

Yes. The second party in a legal case can be set to Employee, Partner (from your contacts), or Others (free text). This makes the module flexible enough to handle a wide range of legal scenarios beyond just employee-related cases.
